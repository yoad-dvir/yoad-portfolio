Who I Am

The Short Version

I interweave philosophy, technology, storytelling, and go-to-market experience to make sense of where cybersecurity is heading. I took the AI pill and I'm deep in the rabbit hole. Not as a tourist, but as someone who believes having a trusted voice in a fast-moving market is the whole game.

The truth is I'm in this for the people. Storytelling is how I think, not a technique I picked up for slides. The best part of any week is the conversation where something finally clicks for someone, and they go do something about it.

Twenty-plus years across Microsoft (~10 years), CyberArk, Secure Islands, IONIX and Silverfort, plus my own startup. I started technical: a graduate of the IDF's elite MAMRAM computing unit, then Officers Course and a Captain's rank. B.A. in Eastern Asia Studies & Business Management from Tel Aviv University, which is also why I still fumble through some Chinese. I've sold, marketed, run global pre-sales, spoken on stage, built communities, and helped take a startup from scrappy to acquired by Microsoft. Now I'm focused on AI Agent Security at Silverfort and running The Identity Underground.
